Topic linking

A log declares a topic — optional; no topic means a general conversation. The conversation that follows the log inherits it instead of guessing, so the archive can answer “what has this person said about nutrition?” across every log they finished. Areas (Earn, Learn, Care, Love, Play) are how Ela web groups topics for display; they are derived, not part of the link. Change a topic on the left and the conversation re-themes in the middle.
